## Live demo flow (booth walkthrough)

1. Greet the visitor, briefly explain ABI is a **research prototype**, not a lending
   decision (point at the disclaimer badge).
2. Either walk them through the real 10-step assessment (if they have ~10–12 minutes) or
   tap **"Load Demo Firm"** to instantly populate one of the seeded demo enterprises
   (`Maundurahuku` or `Zvemuchando`, see `24_SEED_DATA.md`) and jump straight to results.
3. Tap **Calculate** — walk through the score hero, dimension radar, strengths/gaps,
   critical gates, and the financing pathway cards.
4. Show the **Bankability Certificate** panel and explain its disclaimer.
5. Offer to email/print the one-page PDF report.
6. Invite them to complete their own real assessment and submit pilot feedback.
